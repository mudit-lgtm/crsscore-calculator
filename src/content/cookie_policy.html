Cookie Policy

What we store today

Only one item: your cookie choice (whether you allowed advertising cookies) and the date you made it. It is kept in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again on every page. We ask again after 6 months.

Calculator inputs never leave your browser. We do not store them, send them to a server, or use them for advertising.

What may be stored once ads are switched on

Ads are not running on this site today. If we switch on Google AdSense in future, and only if you choose "Accept" or turn on "Advertising" in Settings, Google and its partners may set advertising cookies to show and measure ads, including ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. If you reject advertising, no ad script is loaded.

How to change your choice

Use the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of any page to reopen the settings and change your choice at any time. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings. Personalised Google ads can be managed at adssettings.google.com.

More details are in our Privacy Policy. Questions: contact@crscalculator.site.
