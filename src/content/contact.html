Frequently Asked Questions

Is this calculator affiliated with IRCC or the Canadian government?

No. CRS Score Calculator is an independent free tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) or any government body.

How accurate is the CRS score calculation?

Our calculators are built on the official IRCC CRS grid and are updated whenever IRCC changes the scoring rules. The result is an estimate — your official score is only confirmed when you create an Express Entry profile on the IRCC portal.

Can I use this tool for official immigration applications?

No. This tool is for informational purposes only. Always verify your score through the official IRCC Express Entry system or consult a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC).

I found a bug — how do I report it?

Use the contact form above and select "Bug report" as the subject. Please include the calculator name, your inputs, and the score you received so we can reproduce and fix the issue quickly.
