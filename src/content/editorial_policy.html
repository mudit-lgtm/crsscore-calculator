Our sources

Everything on this site is written from official IRCC pages and official Government of Canada notices: the Comprehensive Ranking System grid on canada.ca, IRCC ministerial instructions and news releases, IRCC's published Express Entry rounds data, and provincial government pages such as ontario.ca and alberta.ca where a program's rules are described. We do not present forum posts, blogs or third-party summaries as sources for scoring rules.

How and when we check for rule changes

We review the site whenever IRCC announces a change to the Express Entry system, and we re-check the calculators against the published CRS grid after each announced change takes effect. Each page carries a "Last updated" date showing when its content was last reviewed.

How draw data is fetched

Draw and cutoff figures on this site are downloaded from IRCC's published Express Entry rounds feed (the official data behind the rounds page on canada.ca). A script rewrites our draw dataset from that feed, and any row we could not confirm against it is marked "unverified" on the page. We do not invent or estimate draw numbers.

Independence

We do not accept payment to change calculator results, to alter a score, or to feature any consultant, school or employer. No paid placement affects what the calculators compute. If that ever changes, this page will say so.

The calculators are estimates

Our calculators reproduce the published IRCC scoring rules as faithfully as we can, but they run in your browser and cannot see your full profile. Results are estimates only — your official CRS score is calculated by IRCC when you submit an Express Entry profile. See our Disclaimer.

Report an error

Found something wrong — a wrong point value, an outdated cutoff, a broken link? Email contact@crscalculator.site with the page, the text and (for calculator errors) your inputs. We correct confirmed errors promptly and update the "Last updated" date on the page.
