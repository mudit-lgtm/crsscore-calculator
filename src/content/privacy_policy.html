Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Home› Privacy Policy
Last updated: 4 November 2026

Welcome to CRS Score Calculator ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit crscalculator.site (the "Site").

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and this site has no user accounts.

1. Information We Collect

Calculator inputs

When you use any CRS calculator on this Site, your inputs (age, education, language scores, work experience and similar details) are processed entirely in your browser. They are not sent to our servers, not stored, and not logged. No calculator on this Site transmits your immigration data anywhere.

Information you choose to send us

The contact form does not submit data to a server. When you press "Send", it opens your own email application with your name, email address, topic and message prefilled, and the email is sent from your email account to contact@crscalculator.site. We receive only what you choose to write, and we use it solely to reply.

Technical data

Like any website, our hosting infrastructure may process standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and to keep the Site available. We do not use this to profile you, and we do not operate analytics tooling on this Site.

2. How We Use Your Information
  • To reply to emails you send us
  • To keep the Site secure and available
  • To fix bugs and improve the calculators
  • To comply with legal obligations if required

We do not use your information for marketing, and we do not send newsletters.

3. Cookies & Advertising

Essential storage

The Site stores minimal information in your browser (for example your calculator selections) so the tools work as expected. This stays on your device.

Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at https://adssettings.google.com and learn more at https://www.aboutads.info. See also https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Third-Party Services

The Site relies on a small number of third parties, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts – web font delivery (font files are fetched by your browser)
  • Google – advertising, if and when advertisements are enabled on the Site
  • canada.ca – we link to official IRCC pages; we are not responsible for their privacy practices
5. Data Storage & Security

The Site is delivered over HTTPS. We do not operate a database of visitor information and we do not store calculator inputs anywhere. Emails you send us are retained in our mailbox only as long as needed to handle your request.

6. Your Rights

EU / UK residents

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to rectification, to erasure, to object to or restrict processing, and to data portability. Our lawful basis for handling an email you send us is your consent and our legitimate interest in replying.

Canada (PIPEDA)

Personal information is collected, used and disclosed only for the purposes described in this policy, with your consent. You may withdraw consent or ask about your information at any time.

California residents

You may request the information we collect about you, its deletion, and confirmation that we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising (we do not sell personal information).

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@crscalculator.site.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always shows the current version.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy: email contact@crscalculator.site or use our Contact page.
