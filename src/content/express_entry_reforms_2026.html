Express Entry Changes November 2026: New CRS Points for High-Wage Occupations

From 3 November 2026, IRCC gives CRS points again for some job offers and Canadian work experience, but only in high-wage occupations. This page explains what is in force, what is only proposed, and how our CRS calculator applies it.

In force from 3 November 2026

Factor2x median wage1.5x median wage1.3x median wage
Canadian work experience in a high-wage occupation (12+ months)704020
Qualifying job offer in a high-wage occupation1007050
  • You get the higher of the two, never both.
  • Maximum for the factor: 150 without a spouse, 140 with a spouse.
  • No LMIA needed.
  • Existing profiles are recalculated. This can take up to two weeks, and invitation rounds pause during that time.

What is a high-wage occupation?

An occupation is high-wage when the median wage of its NOC is at least 1.3x the average median wage of the Canadian workforce, based on Job Bank, Labour Force Survey and Census data from the last three years (2023–2025 for the first list). It depends on the occupation, not on your own salary. If an occupation changes level after you receive an invitation, IRCC keeps the level from the invitation date.

Worked examples

  1. 5 years of Canadian work experience in a 2x occupation, no job offer: +70.
  2. A job offer in a 2x occupation with no Canadian experience: +100.
  3. 1 year of experience in a 1.3x occupation and a 1.3x job offer: the higher of 60 and 50, so 60.

What you should do

  • Check whether your NOC is on IRCC's high-wage occupation list.
  • Update your Express Entry profile with your job-offer details.
  • Remember that extra points do not guarantee an invitation.

Frequently asked questions

Do job offers give CRS points again?

Yes, from 3 November 2026, but only qualifying job offers in high-wage occupations: 100, 70 or 50 points depending on the occupation's wage level.

Do I need an LMIA?

No. The new high-wage points do not require an LMIA.

Can I get points for both work experience and a job offer?

No. You get the higher of the two, capped at 150 without a spouse or 140 with a spouse.

Will the cutoff change?

It may. IRCC has not said how the new points will affect cutoffs. Scores of everyone in the pool are recalculated, and each round's cutoff depends on its category and the number of candidates it invites. Check the latest draws on our cutoff scores page.
